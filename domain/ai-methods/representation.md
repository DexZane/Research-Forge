# Representation

## Diagnostic Questions

- Which local/global, multi-scale, hierarchical, spectral, temporal, or semantic information is absent or inaccessible?
- Is the bottleneck capacity, resolution, tokenization, receptive field, aliasing, compression, or cross-modal alignment?
- Does a probe/oracle show information exists before the decision layer?
- Is the effect explainable by parameter count or pretraining?

## Evidence Targets

Layerwise probes, feature separability, energy/similarity, resolution controls, information-preserving or oracle access, controlled capacity, and stratified outcomes.

## Confounders

Probe validity, linear accessibility versus actual usability, architecture/training changes, dimensionality, and checkpoint selection.

## Stitching Risks

Attention, Mamba, convolution, token mixer, or fusion names do not establish a representation mechanism. Identify the information relation and changed mathematical object.
