# Multimodal

## Diagnostic Questions

- Is failure alignment, grounding, fusion, missing modality, modality dominance, timing, or negative transfer?
- Does each modality contribute unique information at the decision point?
- Is improvement due to more data/pretraining rather than cross-modal mechanism?
- What happens under controlled modality corruption/removal?

## Evidence Targets

Modality ablation/corruption, alignment measures, cross-modal retrieval/grounding, controlled data/pretraining, missing-modality tests, fusion-layer probes, and subgroup analysis.

## Confounders

Unequal encoders, pretraining corpora, token budgets, data leakage, modality quality, and extra parameters.

## Stitching Risks

“Fuse X and Y” is a combination gap. Require a necessary cross-modal dependency and prediction beyond more information/capacity.
