# Assignment and Matching

Applies to detection, DETR, mixture-of-experts routing, correspondence, bipartite matching, sampling, positive selection, supervision allocation, and O2O/O2M.

## Diagnostic Questions

- Does a valid candidate/correspondence exist (coverage/ceiling)?
- Does the rule select it reliably (regret/switch rate/quality)?
- Is matching stable across scale, density, class, noise, and training time?
- Does training assignment align with inference semantics?
- Is failure due to representation/objective rather than matching?

## Evidence Targets

Candidate coverage/ceiling, selection regret, switch rate, assignment entropy, positive quality, oracle matching, controlled candidate count, gradient allocation, and subgroup outcomes.

## Confounders

Proposal quality, resolution, augmentation, label ambiguity, classification/localization calibration, number of queries/experts, and post-processing.

## Stitching Risks

“Scale-aware,” “quality-aware,” or “dynamic matching” terminology is saturated across families. Search historical synonyms and isolate the new reliability prediction.
