# Hypothesis Schema

## Required Fields

`H-` ID, candidate/version, H0–H3 ladder level, method-free statement, observation gap, known mechanism claims, missing link, scope, mechanism chain, predictions, competing hypotheses, falsifiers, evidence, epistemic/lifecycle status, confidence, preregistration version, and supersession.

## Validity

- Epistemic status remains `HYPOTHESIS`.
- Statement contains no proposed method as its explanatory cause.
- Known A/B relations link evidence-backed claims; missing links are explicit unknown/hypothesis edges.
- Active hypothesis has at least one prediction, one falsifier, and 2–4 credible alternatives.
- H3 requires prior H0–H2 support and interventional design; it cannot be inferred directly from correlation.
- Post-result explanations are new versions marked in decision history; they cannot satisfy earlier preregistration retroactively.
- Use `SUPPORTED_NOT_PROVEN`, never `VERIFIED`.

## Prediction Invariants

Primary predictions specify construct, operationalization, condition, expected true/false pattern, diagnostic/alternative metrics, confounders, falsifier, and decision impact. Numeric thresholds require rationale as project criteria.
