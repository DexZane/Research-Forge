# Researchability, Fit, and Opportunity Schema

## Research Question Canvas

Required: `RQ-` ID, phenomenon/status, unit/condition, knowledge gap, mechanism question, observable outcome, minimum discriminating path, three-level scope ladder, stop/reframe condition, evidence/debt IDs, lifecycle, and version.

Validity:

- Phenomenon and knowledge gap are distinct; an untried module or score target is not either.
- Minimum path names an observation, not a full publication program.
- Every unknown decision-critical field has reasoning debt and an action.
- The canvas may guide a candidate but does not prove novelty or mechanism.

## Fit Card

Required: `FIT-` ID, hard constraints, preferences, capability gaps, dependency owners, verified/assumed availability, minimum/core/extension feasibility, canonical preflight outcome, lifecycle, and version.

Validity:

- Separate `HARD_CONSTRAINT`, `PREFERENCE`, and `ASSUMPTION`.
- Use only the researchability preflight outcomes defined in `schemas/ids-and-enums.md`.
- A missing skill/resource creates a gap or `HOLD_RESOURCE`; it does not invalidate a question scientifically.
- Human preference/mentor advice is recorded as input, never as evidence.

## Opportunity Signal

Required: `OP-` ID, signal type, source/provenance, bounded observation/status, scope, alternative explanations, verification action, linked RQ/candidate IDs, evidence/debt IDs, lifecycle, and version.

Validity:

- `DISCOVERY` or `VERIFICATION_PENDING` signals cannot support a formal candidate gap.
- A verified signal requires an appropriate source/read/evidence link.
- At least one alternative explanation is required for active signals.
