# S06 — Candidate Portfolio

## Purpose

Generate a broad, mechanistically diverse candidate pool without prematurely choosing a winner.

## Entry Conditions

Landscape, matrix, red-ocean map, and generation constraints exist.

## Reads

All S02–S05 reports/registries, selected `BL-` baseline contract, RQ canvas, FIT card, verified opportunity signals, user interests as constraints, killed archive/genealogy.

## Writes

Candidate records, proposed innovation signatures, draft core commitments, and genealogy. Exploration mode targets 8–15; validation mode includes original, refined, and alternative-mechanism candidates.

## Required Questions

Which verified opportunity signal and RQ canvas support this candidate? What exact bounded delta does it make from the selected baseline contract while preserving a fair comparison? What precise research question, bottleneck, operation, changed object, critical condition, observable contrast, closest known work, risks, planned falsifier, and falsification budget does each candidate have? Does the portfolio span data, representation, assignment, optimization, training, inference, generalization, or efficiency?

## Required Actions

Express candidates as problems/mechanism questions, not module names; link each candidate to the active `BL-` profile, an RQ canvas, and at least one verified opportunity signal; write a bounded baseline delta statement; create a proposed signature and draft `CM-` commitment that pins the baseline contract version; reject vague signatures; enforce diversity; label novelty `UNVERIFIED`; revive killed ancestors only with new evidence, scope, or mechanism.

## Required Protocols

[Reasoning](../protocols/reasoning.md), [Researchability](../protocols/researchability.md), [Opportunity Signals](../protocols/opportunity-signals.md), [Hypothesis](../protocols/hypothesis.md), [Innovation Signature](../protocols/innovation-signature.md), [Commitment Integrity](../protocols/commitment-integrity.md), [Novelty](../protocols/novelty.md), AI-method domain modules.

## Parallelizable Work

Candidate generation by distinct mechanism families after shared constraints are frozen.

## Sequential Work

Deduplicate, enforce diversity, build genealogy, and register centrally.

## Required Outputs

Candidate registry, proposed signature/commitment registry, idea genealogy, `reports/candidate-portfolio.md`.

## Exit Conditions

Required portfolio size/diversity, stable IDs, baseline/RQ/signal provenance, complete proposed signatures, draft commitments with predictions/falsifier/budget assumptions, and no module-only candidates; route S07.

## Rollback Conditions

Candidate set exposes missing landscape line: S02–S05 as appropriate.

## Kill Conditions

Candidates may be withdrawn for incoherence but S07 formalizes cheap kills.

## Forbidden Actions

Do not select a champion, assert novelty, or preserve the user’s original idea without alternatives.

## Gate Behavior

No gate.
