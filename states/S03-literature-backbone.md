# S03 — Literature Backbone

## Purpose

Establish foundation, milestones, mainstream, recent frontier, and emerging preprints for each research line.

## Entry Conditions

Landscape research lines and concept dictionary exist.

## Reads

Scope, landscape, paper/search registries, bibliography registry, evidence ledger, cutoff.

## Writes

Canonical paper families, verified citation metadata, research-line backbone, reading tiers, citation chains, and coverage debt.

## Required Questions

For each line: who/what founded it, which milestones changed the mechanism, what is mainstream, what is recent, and what debate remains?

## Required Actions

Search by mechanism and historical terminology; group paper families; perform citation chaining for anchors; upgrade formal backbone papers to at least R2.

## Required Protocols

[Search](../protocols/search.md), [Bibliography](../protocols/bibliography.md), [Reading](../protocols/reading.md), [Evidence](../protocols/evidence.md), [Integrity](../protocols/integrity.md).

## Parallelizable Work

Different research lines and citation chains; central paper-family merge is sequential.

## Sequential Work

Discovery → family normalization → anchor R2 → backbone commit.

## Required Outputs

`reports/literature-backbone.md`, paper and bibliography registry updates, citation lineage, coverage/debt records.

## Exit Conditions

Every active line answers foundation/current/recent/debate and critical anchors are R2; route S04.

## Rollback Conditions

Missing line returns S02; foundational terminology gap continues S03.

## Kill Conditions

None.

## Forbidden Actions

Do not treat search snippets/surveys as primary evidence or list papers without research-line organization.

## Gate Behavior

No gate.
