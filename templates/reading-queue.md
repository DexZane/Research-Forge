## Rules

- Include only paper IDs that resolve to a project paper record; link uncertainty to the registry rather than inventing metadata here.
- Use `IMMEDIATE`, `NEXT`, `BACKGROUND`, or `DEFERRED` priority.
- State why the paper matters to a gate, threat, claim, hypothesis, baseline, diagnostic, or implementation choice.
- Suggested tags are workflow hints only. Do not imply that a BibTeX import has created a Zotero collection, downloaded full text, or verified scientific content.
- Keep `references.bib` as citation metadata only; keep Research Forge evidence and decision state in the project registry.
