# Human Collaboration Protocol

## Purpose

Make G1/G2 discussion with a supervisor, mentor, or collaborator concrete without treating authority, preference, or career advice as scientific evidence.

## Discussion Packet

When the user names a mentor/collaborator or requests feedback, produce a `human-discussion-packet.md` alongside the normal gate packet. It contains:

- the requested decision and deadline;
- RQ canvas and scope ladder;
- the top 1–3 opportunity signals and their evidence limits;
- candidate alternatives, strongest threats, and minimum path;
- verified/unverified resource and capability gaps;
- specific questions the human is uniquely positioned to answer;
- decisions, constraints, and unresolved disagreements to log.

## Human Input Boundary

Classify human input as one of:

- `CONSTRAINT_DECISION`: budget, scope, deadline, access, or team allocation;
- `DOMAIN_JUDGMENT`: a proposed interpretation requiring later source verification;
- `MENTORSHIP_ADVICE`: skill, sequencing, or career advice;
- `ARTIFACT_POINTER`: a source, dataset, codebase, or contact to verify.

Only a verified artifact can become research evidence. Record the rest as human input with provenance and scope. Do not invent a supervisor, seek external contact, or imply approval without the user's explicit action.

## Gate Integration

At G1, ask questions that materially change the RQ canvas or FIT card. At G2, ask questions that decide portfolio priority, minimum path, or team/resource commitment. The user remains the gate authority unless they explicitly delegate that authority in the project record.
