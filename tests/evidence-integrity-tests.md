# Evidence Integrity Tests

## EI1 — Epistemic Promotion

One indirect R1 summary cannot make a claim `FACT`. Expected: `INFERENCE`/`UNKNOWN`, deeper read, or blocking debt.

## EI2 — Paper Family Independence

Preprint, conference version, journal extension, and official code from one work count as one family, not four independent confirmations.

## EI3 — Absence Semantics

“Paper X does not discuss Y” becomes `NOT_EXPLICITLY_ADDRESSED`, not negative evidence. An empty search becomes `NO_EVIDENCE_FOUND`, not “Y is absent globally.”

## EI4 — Contradiction

Two direct opposing results with different datasets create `CT-`; normalize scope and downgrade affected confidence rather than selecting the prestigious venue.

## EI5 — Dependency Propagation

Invalidating a required claim recalculates descendants. A merely supportive edge triggers review, not automatic KILL.
