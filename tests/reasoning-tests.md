# Reasoning Tests

## R1 — Gap versus Novelty

An empty matrix intersection is only `CANDIDATE_GAP`; novelty remains unverified until adversarial search and peeling.

## R2 — Hypothesis before Method

Given “add dynamic matching,” expected output first states observation, known relations, missing link, method-free hypothesis, predictions, alternatives, and falsifiers.

## R3 — Evidence Insufficient versus False

No reliable evidence for a mechanism produces LOW confidence/UNKNOWN or HOLD, not automatic KILL for falsity.

## R4 — Simpler Explanation

If stronger augmentation explains the gain under matched tests, weaken the method mechanism claim and record negative evidence.

## R5 — Confidence

High novelty confidence cannot compensate for low evidence/coverage; each confidence dimension remains separate with rationale.
